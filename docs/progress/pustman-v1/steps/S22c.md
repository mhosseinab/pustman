## What shipped
- `panel.rs`: the `Panel` trait (`panel_id`, `title`, `icon`, `position`; focus comes from `Focusable`), the type-erased `PanelHandle` for `Entity<T: Panel>`, and `Workspace::register_panel`, which routes a panel to its dock by position.
- `dock.rs`: a dock holds `Vec<Box<dyn PanelHandle>>` plus the active index, and draws a selector strip with one `ui::Button` per panel above the active panel. Entry ids and selectors are `dock-panel-<panel_id>`. Clicking an entry activates the panel and focuses it. `set_panel` is removed (it had no callers).
- `workspace.rs`: two lines (`mod panel;` and `pub use`).

## Decisions
- Re-registering a panel id replaces its entry in place and keeps the selection.
- Entries use `ui::Button`, not `Tab`, because `Tab`'s activation is crate-private to `TabBar`.
- Activating an unknown id does nothing.
- Registration never moves keyboard focus.
