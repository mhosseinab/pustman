## Findings history
Review probes mutated 4 separate things and each test failed for the right reason (a rebinding, a
duplicate entry in one context, an unresolvable action name, an action renamed in source). 3 LOW:
`load_default_keymap` returns problem strings with no caller yet; duplicate (context, keystroke)
pairs survive parsing and silently shadow at dispatch; the committed `Cargo.lock` was stale.

## Learnings
- Probing is what separates "the tests exist" from "the tests discriminate". Four mutations, four
  right-reason failures.
- A loader that documents "problems so they can be reported" and then never reports them has
  deferred the bug, not avoided it.

## Deferred

the caller surfaces them to the user. S22 must toast or console-log them — dropping the `Vec<String>`
makes a bad bundled entry fail silently in the app. Issues #12, #13, #14.
