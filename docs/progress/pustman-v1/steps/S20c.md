## What shipped

- `TextArea`: a multi-line editor over the shared buffer, with row layout and hit testing.
- `set_highlights(Vec<(Range<usize>, HighlightStyle)>)`: ranges are remapped through edits by `mapped(offset, Boundary, …)`. On a zero-length insert at an offset, a Start boundary moves with the inserted text and an End boundary stays where it is.
- `offset_for_hit_outside_text`: one shared clamp for `TextInput` and `TextArea`. A click above the text goes to 0; a click below it goes to the end of the text.

## Decisions

- A hit exactly on the bottom edge counts as below the text.
- A hit above the text in `TextArea` clamps to offset 0.
- The end-boundary mirror assertion is folded into the F1 test (#93 tracks adding a dedicated test).
