## What shipped

- `crates/response_viewer/src/find.rs`: the find bar for Pretty and Raw bodies. Matching uses the `regex` crate, with plain-text queries escaped and a case toggle. It shows a "3 of 12" count, next and previous wrap and scroll into view, and an invalid regex shows its error inline.
