## What shipped
- The `ui` prelude: Button, IconButton, Label, Icon, Tab and TabBar, Table, Toggle, Tooltip, Modal frame, Toast, Badge and Divider. Colors come only from `cx.theme()`, and every interactive control is focusable and activates by keyboard (synthesized click on Enter and Space).
- The 16x16 Lucide-derived icon set under `assets/icons/` (ISC LICENSE), served through an AssetSource.
- `FontSizesSetting` is a narrow `Setting` view registered in `ui::init`. It fixes the panic from reading an unregistered `Settings`.
- `units.rs` (`PIXELS_PER_REM`, `px_to_rems`) was rewritten independently after round 2 flagged a near-copy of Zed's GPL file.
- TabBar is the single activation path for mouse and keyboard (`activate_tab`). `Tab::on_activated` is `pub(crate)`.
