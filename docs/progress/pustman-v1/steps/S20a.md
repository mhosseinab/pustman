## What shipped
- New crate `text_input` (`[lib] path = src/text_input.rs`): a gap `Buffer` with grapheme and word boundaries from `unicode-segmentation` (new workspace dep), and a single-line `TextInput` entity implementing `EntityInputHandler` (UTF-16 ranges, IME marked text), placeholder, read-only, `Changed`/`Submitted` events, mouse click and drag-select.
- `text_input::init` binds the widget's keys under the `TextInput` key context.

## Decisions
- `set_text` emits `Changed`; Enter emits `Submitted` even when read-only.
- Single-line input turns CR/LF into spaces.
- The layout cache is `RefCell`/`Cell` written in paint (no entity update in paint).
- `ShapedLine::paint` failure is logged (render path, nothing to surface).
- AGENTS.md says `SharedString::from_static`; the pinned gpui has `new_static` (doc drift).
