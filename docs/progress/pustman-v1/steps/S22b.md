## What shipped
- `item.rs`: the `Item` trait (title, dirty, `serialize_draft`) and the type-erased `ItemHandle`.
- `pane.rs` + `pane_tabs.rs`: a pane draws its items as a `ui::TabBar` strip. It can activate, close (button or `CloseTab`), reorder (`move_item`), pin and show a dirty dot. Pinned tabs sort first. Closing the active tab hands activation to its right neighbour, else its left, else Welcome.
- `welcome.rs`: a Welcome item, which `NewTab` opens until `request_editor` lands.
- `workspace.rs`: the `CloseTab`, `NewTab`, `NextTab` and `PreviousTab` delegates.

## Decisions
- `CloseTab` does nothing on a pinned tab, and a pinned tab has no close button.
- `move_item` is not wired to drag: `ui::Tab` exposes no drag hook (crates/ui is outside this step's edit set).
- Stale `TabBar` echo events are dropped, which gives last-writer-wins semantics. Re-activating the active tab is a no-op, so echoes cannot ping-pong; the review's probe confirmed this.
- `SharedString::new_static` is used because it is the real API at the pinned GPUI rev. AGENTS.md's `from_static` is stale.
