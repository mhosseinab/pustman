## What shipped

- `text_input/src/completion.rs` is the hook:
  - It detects the trigger: `{{` before the caret plus the partial name typed after it.
  - It inserts through the one existing edit path.
  - `input.rs` gains two methods that only forward calls: `completion()` and `insert_completion()`. The worker brief allowed this; it is out of the spec's literal edit set (#191).
- `request_editor/src/variable_autocomplete.rs` is the popover:
  - Names come from every scope, de-duplicated, narrowest scope first; names only, never values.
  - The query match is a case-insensitive substring.
  - Keys: Up/Down move the selection, Enter inserts, Escape closes. Inserting writes `name}}`, or just `name` when `}}` already follows the caret.
- `url_bar.rs` gets a minimal attach. Only the URL bar is wired so far; params, headers and body are left to later wiring.
- Known LOW (#192): completing with the caret in the middle of a name leaves the characters after the caret in place.
