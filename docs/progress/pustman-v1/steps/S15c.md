## What shipped

- `ReqwestTransport::with_cookie_jar(Arc<Mutex<CookieJar>>)`. reqwest's own cookie store stays off.
- `cookie_transport::apply_cookie_header` runs before every hop and `capture_cookies` after every hop, the final response included. The jar does all parsing and matching.
- The jar's header is replaced per hop, never stacked. Existing cross-origin stripping in `headers_for_redirect` drops `Cookie` on an origin change.
- `SendSettings::disable_cookie_jar` turns off both apply and capture.

## Decisions

- A user-supplied `Cookie` header wins, and the jar adds nothing alongside it (the same rule as `wire_auto_headers`).
- The sent header lives in the transport's header list. Surfacing it to callers is carried forward to S31b (#94).
- New test helper `cookie_test_support.rs`, per D9 (#95).
