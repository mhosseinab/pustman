## What shipped

`console::ConsolePanel` is the bottom-dock view over the `ConsoleStore` ring buffer:
- Filters: level, protocol and text.
- Clear and pause.
- Rows expand from the keyboard to show their raw, redacted detail.

The panel is not wired into the app yet; that is S32w.
