## What shipped

- `request_editor/src/variable_hover.rs` is the hover popover. It shows:
  - the variable's name and resolved value
  - the winning scope
  - unresolved and secret states
- Secret values are read from the keychain off the foreground thread, through `SecretSources` (store + `SecretKeyLookup`).
  - The default sources have no store and no key, so nothing touches the OS keychain until the app wires its stores.
  - Nothing logs values.
- `url_bar.rs` gets a hover attach. Only the URL bar is wired; the other fields are left to later wiring.
- `text_input/src/input.rs` exposes `index_for_mouse_position` as a read-only accessor. The brief allowed this escape hatch.
