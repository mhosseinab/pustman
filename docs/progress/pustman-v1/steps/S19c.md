## What shipped
- `tab_bar_cycles_with_keyboard`: real key path (focus, then `right`/`left`). Right walks 0→1→2→0 and Left wraps from 0 to 2, with exactly one `Selected` event per move.
- `toast_auto_dismisses_after_timeout`: on the GPUI clock, a 5 s toast is still shown at 4 s and gone at 6 s. An untimed toast stays until dismissed. A toast dismissed by hand cancels its timer and never fires again.
- No production behavior changed: both behaviors already existed from S19a.

- The step text's `components::tab_bar::` → PASS 1 predates S19a's three tab-bar tests. The honest figure is PASS 4.
