## What shipped
- `editing.rs`: a bounded undo stack of transactions (text and selection before and after). Typing coalesces into one step. The run breaks on cursor motion, on a switch between deleting and inserting, on paste or cut, and on undo or redo.
- `input.rs`: copy, cut, paste, undo and redo actions. Every mutation goes through one `edit` path. An IME composition is recorded as one transaction.
- `text_input.rs`: key bindings under the text-input key context: cmd/ctrl-c/x/v/z and cmd/ctrl-shift-z, plus ctrl-y outside macOS.

## Decisions
- `MAX_TRANSACTIONS = 1000`. Past that, the oldest transaction is dropped.
- `set_text` clears undo and redo, because it is a programmatic replacement and not a user edit.
- Pasted CRLF, CR and LF each become one space in a single-line input.
- In read-only mode, cut, paste, undo and redo do nothing. Copy still works.
- The step's Verify pins `PASS 2`. The 12 extra tests each prove a recorded decision.
