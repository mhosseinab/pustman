- `retention.rs`: `prune_once` calls storage's `HistoryRepo::prune_older_than` at most once, after the history insert, inside the writer's lock scope on its background task. No new SQL and no date math.

## Decisions

- The gate is claimed before the delete, so a failed prune is not retried within the session. `0` disables pruning.
- The caller passes the retention days as a value; `http_engine` does not read `settings`.
