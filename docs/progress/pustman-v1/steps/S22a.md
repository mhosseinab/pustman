## What shipped
- A new `workspace` crate with these parts:
  - `Workspace`: a left dock (248 px) and a bottom dock, both open on start; a center `Pane`; a 28 px `StatusBar`.
  - `ToggleSidebar` and `ToggleConsole`, from `pustman_actions`, are handled under the `"Workspace"` key context.
- `StatusBar::add_left_item` and `StatusBar::add_right_item` place items in two groups that tests can select (`status-bar-left`, `status-bar-right`). Each item's debug selector comes from its entity id, so tests prove the items really render on the correct side.
- `Workspace::new(window, cx)` focuses itself. The shortcuts work on a fresh window, and `test_new_workspace_takes_keyboard_focus` pins this.

## Decisions
- `Workspace::new` focuses itself, rather than leaving the window creator to do it. The reason: S22e's wiring step never mentions focus, so a contract left to the caller would be missed.
- The bottom dock's 200 px height is interim. The design pack gives no height for it.
- `init` is a no-op for now, which pinned decision 2 allows.
